Welcome to Kristan

Small enough to feel personal, comfortable enough to feel like home

Kristan is a boutique building of just ten apartments in Elenite, on the quiet northern tip of the Sveti Vlas bay. Wake up to sea air, walk a few steps down to Kozluka Beach, and come back to a bright, freshly renovated apartment with everything you need for a slow holiday.

Every apartment has its own kitchenette, private bathroom and terrace or balcony — some with views straight over the water. Parking is free and right on site, so you can arrive, unload and forget about the car.

Is parking really free?

Yes. Private parking on site is free for all guests, and you don't need to reserve a space in advance.

How far is the beach?

Kozluka Beach is just a few steps from the building — no car or crossing busy roads needed.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes, we can arrange a transfer from Burgas Airport for an extra charge. Let us know your flight details when you book.

Is there a pool?

We don't have a pool — instead, the sea is right on our doorstep, and Action Aquapark is a 15-minute drive away.
